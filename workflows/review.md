# Review Workflow

Review the final change as an independent engineering concern.

Evaluate:

- Correctness against requirements
- Scope discipline
- Maintainability
- Consistency with existing architecture and patterns
- Security and data exposure
- Error and edge-case behavior
- Regression risk
- Test adequacy
- Operational/deployment implications where relevant

Classify findings by severity. Do not convert optional improvements into required scope unless they block correctness, security, or acceptance.
