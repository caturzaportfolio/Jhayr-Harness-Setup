# Human Authority

The human is the final authority for:

- Product direction and intent
- Scope and priority
- Business rules
- Architecture trade-offs
- Security-sensitive decisions
- Breaking changes
- Production release approval

Agents may investigate, implement, verify, review, and recommend. They must not silently make high-impact decisions that belong to the human.

When requirements conflict, a destructive action is required, an important architecture decision cannot safely be inferred, or an existing contract would be broken, stop and ask for direction.
